Privacy Policy

DraftSync · Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Who we are

DraftSync ("the App") is a personal tool operated by Trey. It helps Trey upload Trey's own video files to Trey's own TikTok drafts inbox using TikTok's Content Posting API. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

Contact: treygrokops@mail.grokbot.com

2. Information we access

When you connect a TikTok account to the App via TikTok OAuth, the App may access:

  • Basic profile / open ID — an identifier TikTok provides so the App knows which account is connected
  • Access token (and related token metadata such as expiry / refresh token, if issued) — so the App can call TikTok's APIs on your behalf

When you use the App to upload content, the App also processes:

  • Video files you provide — transmitted to TikTok for the purpose of creating drafts in your account
  • Optional related text you supply for your own use (such as draft captions or hashtags), if that feature is enabled

The App does not ask for your TikTok password. Authentication is handled by TikTok's OAuth screens.

3. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account connection
  • Upload your content to your TikTok drafts via TikTok's Content Posting API
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the App for that purpose

We do not sell your data. We do not share your videos, tokens, or profile identifiers with third parties for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

4. Sharing

Your video files are sent to TikTok as required to create drafts in your account. TikTok's handling of that content is governed by TikTok's own terms and privacy policy.

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the App — and only to the extent necessary.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens and related credentials are stored securely and used only for authorized API calls. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect tokens and files in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Video files are processed for upload; we do not retain them longer than needed to complete the upload and any short-term troubleshooting you request.

6. Retention

We retain connection data (such as open ID and tokens) only while your account remains connected or as needed to provide the App. You can end retention by disconnecting the App.

7. Your choices and revocation

You may revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok account settings (authorized apps / permissions). After revocation, the App will no longer be able to call TikTok APIs on your behalf. You may also email us to request deletion of locally stored connection data.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. International / third-party platforms

Because uploads go to TikTok, your content and related data may be processed on TikTok's infrastructure in accordance with TikTok's policies and applicable law.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a revised version at this URL and updating the effective date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: treygrokops@mail.grokbot.com
