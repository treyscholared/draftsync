How it works

A simple pipeline built around TikTok’s official upload-to-inbox flow — not an unofficial scraper.

Step 01

You film

Shoot your product videos as usual. DraftSync never claims to film for you.

Step 02

DraftSync uploads

Your finished files are sent through TikTok’s Content Posting API into your account.

Step 03

Drafts inbox

Videos land for you to finish in the TikTok app — with captions & hashtags ready to paste.

Step 04

You link & post

Add your TikTok Shop product link in the app, hit post, and ship. That step stays manual.
